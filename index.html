My Mission
Middle school is more powerful than anyone tells you.

I'm building a platform for middle school students who want to do more — compete, explore, achieve — but don't know where to start. No one hands you the roadmap. I want to change that.

Academic competitions like Pentathlon and Decathlon are some of the best-kept secrets in education. They're challenging, exciting, and will take you places you never imagined — literally. I went from my classroom to competing internationally at 13.

This isn't about being a "nerd." It's about being ambitious, curious, and strategic about your own potential. Let's build something great.
